## 1. Problem - What problem are we solving?

Pop & Drop Vending is a real Orlando, FL vending machine company. Before this
site, the business had no dedicated web presence for potential host locations
to learn about the service or request a machine. This site is the company's
primary web presence: it explains the offering and captures leads from
businesses that want a vending machine installed on-site.

## 2. Users - Who is this for?

Decision-makers at businesses/properties considering hosting a vending
machine - offices, apartment complexes, gyms, warehouses, schools, and
hospitals (the location types shown on the site). Secondary audience: the
Pop & Drop team, who receive lead notifications from form submissions.

## 3. Features - What does the MVP need?

Already shipped (see build-plan.md for the checked list):

- Responsive landing page with mobile-first nav and hamburger menu
- Hero section
- About/FAQ accordion content
- "Why choose us" section
- Service-area location cards (by location type)
- Multi-field lead capture form with validation, phone formatting, loading
  state, success confirmation, Supabase persistence, and EmailJS
  notification
- Footer with payment-method badges

## 4. Data - What are we storing?

- `leads` table (Supabase/Postgres): `company_name`, `name`, `email`,
  `phone`, `reason`, `message`, `created_at` (set by the database, not sent
  from the client).

No other persisted data (no auth, no user accounts, no CMS).

## 5. Tech - What stack are we using?

- React 19 + Vite 7 (JavaScript, not TypeScript)
- Tailwind CSS v4, CSS-first config (`@import "tailwindcss"` in `index.css`,
  no `tailwind.config.js`)
- Supabase (`@supabase/supabase-js`) for the `leads` table
- EmailJS (`@emailjs/browser`) for lead-notification emails
- `motion` (Framer Motion successor) and a custom scroll-effect hook for
  section animation
- `react-icons` for iconography
- ESLint (flat config) with `react-hooks` and `react-refresh` plugins
- No backend/API layer - the frontend talks to Supabase and EmailJS directly
  using client-side env vars (`VITE_SUPABASE_URL`, `VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`,
  `VITE_EMAILJS_SERVICE_ID`, `VITE_EMAILJS_TEMPLATE_ID`,
  `VITE_EMAILJS_PUBLIC_KEY`)

## 6. Monetize - How will this make money?

The site itself doesn't transact. The business makes money by placing
vending machines at partner/host locations and taking a revenue share of
on-site vending sales. This site's job is lead generation for that placement
pipeline, not direct monetization.

## 7. UI/UX - How should this look and feel?

Warm, professional small-business aesthetic: amber accent color on a
gray/white base, `font-serif` for headings and form copy, rounded-2xl cards
with soft shadows, scroll-triggered fade/slide-in animations per section
(`useScrollEffect`), mobile-first responsive layout.
